Customer focused

Our commitment

Digital Gloss provide clear and professional guidance without all the technical terminology. We believe that building your online presence should be an exciting experience causing minimal disruption to you.

  • Listen

    Our first no-obligation discussion involves getting to know your business, goals and audience. Every project is tailored to our client’s needs.

  • Communication

    It’s important that you always know what is happening. We make sure that we communicate clearly and often with you.

  • Focus

    We focus on results. Every opportunity reflects on our reputation, and it’s our goal to have you achieving results online. We tie our work to measurable outcomes.

  • Transparency

    We are in this for the long haul. Trust is important to us, and we always maintain full transparency about pricing, timelines and deliverables.

  • Post-launch support

    We stick around after the launch. Support, updates and ongoing improvements are part of the relationship. The work doesn’t end the day you go live.
