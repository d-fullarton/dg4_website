Our services

A website to be found, marketing to be seen, and a brand to be remembered. Choose one service or combine all three. We’ll explain every step in plain English and shape the work around your goals, your customers and your budget.

1 Website development 2 Marketing 3 Branding

Website development

Your website is often the first impression of your business, so it needs to look right, load quickly and make it easy for people to get in touch. We design and build it around your goals and your customers, then look after it once it’s live.

Not sure which type of website you need? Start with a simple domain website, a set of pages with your own web address, ideal when your content rarely changes. If you’d like to update your own content or sell online, we’ll build on a Content Management System (“CMS”), with an e-commerce store if you need one. We’ll recommend what fits and explain why.

  • Planning and design shaped around your audience
  • Building that works on phones, tablets and computers
  • Your web address, secure hosting and fast loading
  • Launch, training and ongoing support, updates and improvements

Marketing

Marketing puts your business in front of the people who are looking for what you offer. We focus on social media, where your customers already spend their time, and build a clear plan around your goals so every post has a purpose.

We start by getting to know your audience, your competition and what makes you different. From there we plan, create and publish content that fits your brand, then look at what’s working and improve it. You’ll always know what’s going out, why, and what it’s achieving. SEO, CTR, CPC… we’ll translate.

  • Research into your audience and competitors
  • A social media strategy and content calendar
  • Photos, graphics and posts made for your brand
  • Posting, paid campaigns and community management
  • Clear reports on measurable results

Branding

Your brand is how people recognise, trust and remember you. We bring your logo, colours, typography, imagery and voice together into one clear story, so your business looks and sounds the same everywhere it appears.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or refreshing an established business, we begin by listening to who you are and who you want to reach. We then design an identity that stands out, speaks to the right customers and is ready to grow with you, online and in print.

  • Brand story, positioning and tone of voice
  • Logo design and variations
  • Colour palette and typography
  • Business cards, letterheads and printed materials
  • Brand guidelines, so everything stays consistent

Bespoke websites, marketing and branding for start-ups, small businesses, and independent consultants.
